AIBOM vs SBOM

SBOM tracks software components. AIBOM extends visibility into models, datasets, prompts, and AI-specific dependencies used in production systems.

What SBOM covers

  • Libraries and package versions
  • Known vulnerability correlation
  • License and provenance data

What AIBOM adds

  • Model origin, version, and trust level
  • Dataset lineage and restrictions
  • Prompt templates and policy controls
  • Embedding stores and retrieval connectors
